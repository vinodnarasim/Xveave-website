FAQ

Assortment intelligence — common questions.

What is AssortVeave?

AssortVeave is an AI-assisted assortment intelligence engine that helps brands and retailers decide which products belong in which stores — and why. It moulds to your own assortment logic instead of forcing every category into fixed grades.

Does it replace my merchandising team?

No. AssortVeave gives your team the why and the what-if behind every decision — people still decide and sign off. Governance and a full audit trail are built in.

Which categories does it suit best?

Any brand steering product through tiered or selective retail. It is sharpest where product is premium and scarce — luxury, footwear, apparel, fragrance — where getting the assortment wrong is most costly.
