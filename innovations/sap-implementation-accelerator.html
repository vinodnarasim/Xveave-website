FAQ

SAP implementation accelerator — common questions.

What is an SAP implementation accelerator?

An SAP implementation accelerator is tooling and method that speeds up an S/4HANA project. DesignVeave accelerates the design phase — capturing workshops, structuring the Business Process Design, and tracing every requirement and RICEF from first workshop to sign-off.

Can AI assist the SAP design and blueprint phase?

Yes. DesignVeave acts as an AI blueprint agent: it refines workshop notes, proposes design solutions, generates process diagrams from plain business language, and drafts test cases and authorizations — with a consultant reviewing and approving every step.

Does DesignVeave align with SAP Activate?

Yes. DesignVeave structures the design and explore phase in line with the SAP Activate methodology, keeping requirements, solutions and RICEF scope traceable through the standard delivery flow.

Is our design data kept private?

Yes. Each project's Business Process Design lives in your own private repository. Your design documents stay in your tenant and are never shared across clients.

Does it support SAP fashion and retail implementations?

DesignVeave is built by a team with deep SAP fashion, retail and distribution experience — where seasonality, assortments and multi-tier distribution stretch the standard process. It suits any S/4HANA program but is especially at home in those industries.

Who is DesignVeave for?

Both SAP system integrators running S/4HANA implementations and enterprises transforming their own SAP landscape — anyone who wants a faster, more structured, fully traceable design phase.
